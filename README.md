# Second Chance Thrift

A Windows first-person thrift-store simulator. Grandpa has retired and handed you his little store. Find secondhand goods, clean and restore them, stock your displays and grow the business.

**441 playable items are included.** The planned collection of 1,001 is still in development; 560 more items remain planned. [Release notes and checksum](https://github.com/Shad0w746/second-chance-thrift-downloads/releases/tag/v0.14.1).

1. Download the ZIP above and choose **Extract All**.
2. Open **SecondChanceThrift.exe** inside the extracted folder.
3. Keep the accompanying `Engine` and `ThriftStore` folders beside it.

Windows 10/11 64-bit with DirectX 11 graphics is the target. Unreal Engine, Blender, Python and OBS do not need to be installed. If Windows reports missing runtime components, run the included `Engine/Extras/Redist/en-us/UEPrereqSetup_x64.exe` and try again. Hardware performance and clean-machine compatibility are still being evaluated.

v0.14.1 adds **Grandpa's miniature thrift shop on its own labeled pedestal** just left of the warehouse entrance. Press E to inspect it. The directory sells a separate stock item, so the reference exhibit stays on display.

This early playtest includes 441 naturally sized goods across thrift, toy, medieval/castle and retro collections, a dimensional warehouse with hand-cranked three-tier shelves and directory filters, a seeded neighborhood, a five-slot carry bar, six shop displays, cleaning/repair/restoration benches and **Bargain Bean**, a silly customer who buys affordable displayed goods. Common through Epic items need no workshop kits or consumables; higher rarities retain their requirements. The window sign opens or closes your shop, with automatic closing at 8 PM. An 8 AM-8 PM day lasts 15 active real minutes.

Use **W/A/S/D** and the mouse to move, hold **Left Shift** to sprint, press **Space** to jump, **E** to interact, **Tab** for inventory, **1-5** to select carried goods (repeat to stow), and **Esc** to pause. The game starts directly; Dev/Admin tools start Off and can be enabled in the Esc menu. Full instructions and a first-sale scenario are in `START-HERE.txt`. To see Bean make a purchase, list a good at or below its estimated value while the shop is open, then close the menu.
